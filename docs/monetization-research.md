# Low-budget monetization proposal

Research checked 28 September 2026. This is a proposal, not implemented paid functionality and not a revenue forecast. The current shop uses earned virtual coins only.

## What the evidence does and does not say

Sensor Tower's 2026 overview describes shrinking mobile downloads and the importance of retaining existing players. This supports testing whether players return before buying acquisition, not assuming more shop inventory guarantees income. [Sensor Tower, State of Gaming 2026](https://sensortower.com/report/state-of-gaming-2026)

Mistplay's 2024 survey of nearly 2,000 adult IAP spenders in the US/Canada reports interest in offers tailored to preferences and warns about churn when spending feels necessary to progress. This is an older, selected spender sample, not evidence that Indian players or this game's audience will buy a particular skin. [Mistplay spender research](https://business.mistplay.com/resources/mobile-gaming-iap-spend-trends-2024)

Google's AdMob guidance describes voluntary ads exchanged for clearly disclosed rewards, with reward value and placement tested. Publisher case studies and vendor claims are not an earnings prediction for this prototype. [AdMob rewarded ads playbook](https://admob.google.com/home/resources/rewarded-ads-playbook/)

## Smallest sensible experiment

These are design hypotheses and suggested India price tests, NOT researched optimal prices. Use localized store pricing if implementing them later.

1. **Choose-your-style starter pack — test ₹99–₹199.** A player-selected companion, arrow skin and board theme, plus five hints. Show the exact contents and animation previews. No duplicate-item charges; choose unowned items or clearly defined replacement value. Preserve current coin-earned ownership and free ways to play.
2. **Optional rewarded hint.** Explicitly offer one hint for one completed rewarded ad. Keep the coin purchase option and free restarts. If ads are unavailable, do not trap the player. No automatic ads after each mistake.
3. **Coordinated cosmetic collections — later, test ₹149–₹299.** A cohesive companion outfit/reactions, arrow effect and board. Create new outfits only after players show demand for the existing set; artwork is a real production cost. Cosmetics have no puzzle advantage.
4. **Extra puzzle chapters — later, test ₹149–₹299.** A complete, clearly counted pack beyond the included 40 + boss. Make and test the content before offering it for sale; do not sell vague promises of future levels.

Do not launch a subscription or battle pass with only a short finite campaign and no proven update schedule. Do not add loot boxes, fake discounts/countdowns, guilt-based companion sales prompts, or deliberately impossible levels. The one-heart stages should remain a fair challenge with unlimited free retries, not a pressure tactic for selling hearts. A remove-ads purchase makes sense only if there are actual interruptive ads to remove and its scope is explained; optional rewarded ads alone do not justify it.

## Protect the budget

Start with a small organic test group (for example 30–50 people; this is qualitative product testing, not enough to establish a reliable purchase conversion rate). Ask which items they want and what they believe the bundle includes. Review next-day/next-week returns, where players abandon levels, and whether the single-heart transition frustrates them. Add a reproducible daily challenge or more free content if players like the game but run out of reasons to return. Do not pay for user acquisition until actual retention and net revenue support the spend. No income is guaranteed; do not depend on this project for urgent bills.

## Before accepting real money

Use the relevant store purchase systems with verified transactions, duplicate-grant protection, durable entitlements and restoration. The current local preference save is not sufficient purchase security or cross-device restoration. Google recommends secure-backend verification before granting entitlement. [Google Play purchase security](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/security)

Apple distinguishes consumables from permanent non-consumables; permanent cosmetic unlocks/chapters and consumable hints should be modeled accordingly. [Apple purchase types](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/configure-in-app-purchase-settings/overview-for-configuring-in-app-purchases/)

Budget for store enrollment, applicable service fees/taxes, any backend, and original/licensed art. Confirm commercial rights to every character asset. Ads also require audience/privacy/consent decisions and proper test configuration before launch. None of these services or expenses were authorized or activated by this update.
